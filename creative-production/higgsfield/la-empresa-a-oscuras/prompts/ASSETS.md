## Location plates

1. office-to-warehouse establishing angle;
2. reverse warehouse-to-office angle;
3. frontal blocking angle at the operational table;
4. overhead/three-quarter angle of the analysis table.

Remove random people, vehicles, labels, clocks and loose props that could mutate. Preserve blank trackable screens and stable door/window positions.

## Props
