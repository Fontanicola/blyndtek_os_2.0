# Master script — 60 seconds

Idioma original: inglés. Voz grave pero humana, contenida, sin tono de tráiler ni locución institucional.

## Voice-over

> They call them small businesses.
>
> But there is nothing small about what they carry.
>
> Before daybreak, orders move. Materials cross cities. Decisions shape everything.
>
> Yet no one can see the whole.
>
> An order becomes a message. A delay becomes a habit. A critical process becomes someone’s memory.
>
> The most advanced companies build technology around their operation.
>
> Growing companies are told to work around the technology.
>
> We believe that should change.
>
> We start with the operation.
>
> We measure it. We make loss visible. Then we build around the business.
>
> Growth should not make a company harder to see.
>
> It should reveal what it can become.
>
> Blyndtek.
>
> See the company you’re becoming.

## Copy en pantalla

No usar titulares durante el cuerpo. Sólo tres intervenciones deterministas:

1. `Measure what is real.`
2. `Build around the business.`
3. End card: `Making complex work simple.`

Antes de masterizar, revisar si las dos primeras líneas aportan o si la película funciona mejor únicamente con imagen y voz.

## End card

- Logo horizontal oficial de Blyndtek.
- `Making complex work simple.`
- Fondo blanco o navy según el frame de salida aprobado.
- Sin precio, URL, botón ni CTA de performance.
