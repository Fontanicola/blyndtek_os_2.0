## What the tests proved

- Restrained, single-axis motion preserves the premium observational language.
- Physical contact survives when the frame contains one operator action and a rigid prop.
- Blank compositing surfaces are reliable enough for deterministic post-production.
- Asking one generated clip to create an exact material match cut invites morphing. The cut belongs in the edit, not inside the generative transition.
