# G2 — asset lock plan

## Objective

Turn the G1 visual language into a reproducible world bible before generating additional motion. G2 does not attempt a rough cut and does not authorize any video, audio, upscale or publication job.

- Three 2K reference-preserving cleanups with Nano Banana Pro.
- Three additional 16:9 location discovery plates with Soul Location.
- Three hero props/blocking frames with Soul Cinematic.
- Three partial-operator blocking frames with Soul Cinematic.

Initial live estimate on 2026-08-25: **7.08 credits**.

## Why this batch is enough

The full shot manifest has eighteen generative shots, but they all reuse the same three locations, three material anchors, three operator blockings and three reusable props. Locking those relationships first prevents spending 17.5-credit video jobs on fake text, unstable architecture or mismatched edges.

## Review gate

After the twelve stills are generated:

1. score every asset for physical realism, geography, light, brand safety and editability;
2. approve one clean master and one supporting angle per world;
3. confirm the food-tray → steel → stretch-film edge system;
4. confirm hands, tools and pallet-jack contact;
5. stop and price G3 motion tests using only approved assets.

No unused part of the eight-credit cap rolls into G3.
