# G3 — motion-family test plan

## Purpose

Test the four motion families that the complete film depends on before generating the remaining production shots:

1. operational geography in a wide;
2. one human action with believable contact and weight;
3. the fragmented-order idea on blank surfaces;
4. the missing food-to-metal half of the hero match-cut system.

The metal-to-pallet half already exists from G1 and is not regenerated here.

## Gate

After generation, every clip receives full temporal QA. Useful time ranges are logged and assembled with the two G1 motion tests into a silent proof animatic. No rerender, audio, upscale, final production or publication is authorized by this batch.
